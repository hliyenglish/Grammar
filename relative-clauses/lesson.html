Relative Clauses

Main Lesson

1. Defining Relative Clauses

These give us essential information to understand who or what is being referred to. We use who for people, which for things, and that for both.

2. Examples

- The woman who lives next door is a doctor.

- Where is the cheese that was in the fridge?
