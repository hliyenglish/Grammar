Present Simple

Main Lesson

1. When to use Present Simple

We use the present simple tense to talk about things in general. We use it to say that something happens all the time or repeatedly, or that something is true in general.

2. Examples

- Nurses look after patients in hospitals.

- I usually go away at weekends.

- The earth goes round the sun.
